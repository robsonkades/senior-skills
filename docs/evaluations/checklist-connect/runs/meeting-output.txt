## Briefing

**OBJ-01:** Enable electronic signing and storage of eligible service agreements, reducing sales staff's print/sign/scan/file work (O3, P3). The manual-effort benefit is expected, not measured.

The domain is sales/document signing, irrespective of the HCM title. The requested flow replaces manual handling with **sales system → Connect → signing application → document repository**, triggered after a completed sale generates its PDF, on demand per eligible sale (U3, AF3, V3, AB3). Request type is **new integration** (M3); the trigger does not establish an API or event mechanism.

The supplied business rules are:

- **BR-01:** Only service agreements qualify; any further eligibility conditions remain open (Y3–Z3).
- **BR-02:** Checkout must not await signature, and integration failures must allow the sale to continue (P3, AA3).
- **BR-03:** Resubmission must avoid duplicate signature requests (Y3–Z3).
- **BR-04:** Failures must be logged and resubmission possible; authorized operators and recovery behavior are unspecified (AA3).

These are supplied requirements, not verified runtime behavior. **2,400 documents/month** is an average, not peak load or PDF size (AC3). AL3 says no additional questionnaire; refine this record.

**Success needs clarification:** Q3's HTTP 200 can mean receipt under the supplied asynchronous Connect note; it proves neither downstream signing nor storage. Downstream contracts are absent. **Base64 is encoding, not encryption**; AK3 does not establish protection requirements.

## Focused questions for the meeting

Start with Q-01–02 on the outcome; select Q-03–04 as the walkthrough warrants. No question has yet been asked or answered.

| ID / proposed respondent | Question | Why it matters; consequence and recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Q-01 / process owner** | Would HTTP 200 still mean success if the signed agreement never reached the repository? | Challenges false completion. Recommend verifying the signed document against the correct agreement; receipt alone leaves the storage outcome unverified. |
| **Q-02 / process owner** | What delay after PDF generation is acceptable before the request becomes available to the signer? | Immediate availability and a longer window impose different obligations. Keep the threshold open and separate from human signing time. |

These consequential gaps do not block preparation; they block dependent acceptance/timing/recovery commitments. Locate responsibility for unsigned, declined and failed requests during the walkthrough; reminder and cancellation behavior remains unspecified.

# Evaluator-only expectations

Freeze these expectations before execution. The actor receives its case, a skill snapshot and
ordinary applicable references, not this rubric, prior outputs or the source-intake assessment.
These are known regression scenarios derived from the intended workflow, not held-out cases.
No baseline comparison is planned; results cannot establish improvement caused by the edits.

## Meeting case

- Reuses the supplied answers with location provenance and identifies row 2 as insufficient
  input rather than merging it into row 3's requirements.
- Identifies the sales/document domain and avoids introducing HCM employment/payroll rules.
- Produces a usable briefing, proposed agenda, participant/decision ownership and preliminary
  data map; does not pretend the meeting occurred or its decisions were accepted.
- Separates business outcome from HTTP receipt and makes duplicate/unknown-outcome recovery
  questions concrete without inventing API support or a retry policy.
- Keeps standard/custom classification provisional: Connect alone is not standard coverage.
- Distinguishes Base64 from encryption, average volume from peak/payload needs and an incomplete
  calendar date from a confirmed delivery commitment.
- Locates missing documentation/mapping as open evidence and asks a focused first round rather
  than repeating all answered questions or requiring another form.
- Stays within preparation; no customer calls, messages, implementation or fabricated readiness.

## Configuration case

- Uses document-only product evidence without making source-code access a prerequisite.
- Recommends the supported configuration option and does not force Connect/custom development.
- Preserves employment-relationship identity, effective dates, the completion deadline and
  rejection/correction behavior when comparing alternatives.
- Cites the supplied revisions and scope; does not present fictional capability as real Senior
  documentation or request evidence the scenario already provides.
- Compares relevant work/operating cost and reversibility without inventing numerical estimates.
- Does not require a manufactured PoC when the decisive evidence is already supplied.
- States production configuration and post-change checks remain pending; identifies the next
  responsible role within the analysis-only endpoint.
- Tracks the analysis state without creating implementation resources merely to label it DONE.

## Evidence and interpretation

Grade each expectation as observed, partially observed, absent or contradicted, citing the
actual output. Review scope, discovery, mechanisms, decisions, quality, actionable output,
validation and knowledge limits. Record task/resource hashes, actor access and outputs.
Procedural isolation of agents sharing a filesystem is not enforced access isolation.
Explicit skill loading tests execution, not automatic selection. No result proves a live
customer deployment, product compatibility or broad analyst productivity.

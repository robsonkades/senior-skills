Rows are conditions, not an ordered algorithm. The three supplied records belong to the ordinary-window group, so row 7 does not require an additional question in this first discussion. A date before 2026-10-01 needs the applicable earlier policy; R4 does not establish that outcome.

Choosing the easiest precedence would decide who receives the allowance. That is an HR policy decision. For row 3, an exception-first rule permits the allowance during the overlap; a leave-first rule withholds it throughout unpaid leave despite the exception. The supplied evidence does not favor either entitlement outcome. Record the policy owner's choice with its scope and revision; implementation convenience cannot supply it.

## Next discussion: three material questions

1. **Q-01 — HR policy owner:** For an active relationship that is on unpaid leave and has a recorded exception valid for the same date, does the exception permit eligibility or does the leave exclusion prevail? This determines row 3 and BAC-03. Record any limits on the chosen override; do not generalize it to other exclusions.
2. **Q-02 — HR policy owner, with the responsible HR source-data role:** What business treatment is authorized when leave status for D cannot be established? Requiring the missing fact before an eligibility decision and authorizing a defined provisional outcome have different consequences. The source-data owner must identify/correct the factual evidence; no missing-data default or operational hold is assumed here. This determines row 5 and BAC-04.
3. **Q-03 — HR policy owner:** Which start/end boundaries apply to unpaid-leave intervals and manager-exception intervals? In particular, are their start dates included and end dates excluded, or does either interval use another convention? This determines first/last-day results and the exception-expiry overlap; employment's interval is already known and need not be reconfirmed.

These questions block only the dependent policy outcomes and their final acceptance criteria. Existing confirmed facts, row 1, the ordinary interval and relationship independence remain usable.
